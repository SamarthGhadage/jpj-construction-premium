Founded in 2001 by principal architect Shailesh Pandita, JPJ Construction brings architecture, construction and interiors into one considered process. We work closely with clients to make ambitious spaces practical, personal and enduring.

From a family home to a commercial environment, every decision is measured against the way a place will feel, function and last.
